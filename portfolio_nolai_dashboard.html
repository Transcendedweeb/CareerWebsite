Nolai Dashboard

Stakeholders: Virtual Reality Learning Lab, Nationaal Onderwijslab AI

Date Finished: July 2025

Languages: Laravel, PHP, HTML/CSS/JS, Unity/C#, Python

GitHub:Not avaible

Overview

As part of the NOLAI project “Woordenschat vergroten door rijke context”, I developed an interactive dashboard that supports researchers in guiding and analyzing Virtual Reality (VR) sessions with young children. The broader project explores how VR and AI can help reduce language gaps in preschoolers by placing them in rich, immersive learning environments.
My contribution focused on solving a major limitation: researchers had little control or visibility during VR sessions. To address this, I designed and built a proof-of-concept dashboard that enables real-time monitoring, recording, and interaction — while ensuring data privacy and usability. The tool is intended as a stepping stone toward scalable, safe, and effective VR-based language education in primary schools.

Features

- Live Monitoring: Watch VR sessions through a live video stream from the headset.
- Session Control: Start, manage, and end VR activities remotely.
- Notes & Annotations: Make time-linked observations directly within the dashboard.
- Recording & Playback: Capture and review audio, video, and in-game interactions for later analysis.
- Secure Data Management: Local storage of all session data, compliant with privacy and safety standards (ISO 9241-210).
- Scalable Architecture: Built with Unity (VR side) and Laravel + MariaDB (dashboard side), designed for future AI integration such as speech recognition and automated analysis.

Challenges & Learnings

Developing the dashboard brought both technical and design challenges. The tight project timeline required me to rapidly prototype and validate features through usability testing with real stakeholders. Balancing usability and data security was critical, especially since the system involves young children.
I learned to apply design thinking and the ISO 9241-210 human-centered design standard in practice, iterating based on continuous researcher feedback. On the technical side, I deepened my skills in Unity development, Laravel backend engineering, and database design with MariaDB. Perhaps the most valuable lesson was how to translate complex user needs into a simple, reliable, and future-proof tool that bridges educational research and technology.
