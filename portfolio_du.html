Dobbelspel Unity

Stakeholders:Personal project

Date Finished: December 2024

Languages: Unity/C#

GitHub: Dobbelspel Unity repo

Overview

Dobbelspel-Unity is a richly immersive version of our family board game tradition, rebuilding the original Python experience in Unity, it combines a traditional dice roll on a custom Arduino-enhanced board that bridges into Unity. Players navigate across the gameboard and play minigames with DIY-controllers connected to the Arduino. Explore a souls-like boss arena, test your nerve with a football penalty showdown, play russian roulette, and react to dynamic event-driven scenarios, each with its own cinematic and audio-driven flair. This upgraded version builds on your Python original to deliver deeper engagement and polished visuals.

Features

- Seamless Physical-Digital Interaction: Arduino-based input brings real-world actions into the digital game.
- Boss Arena: A challenging souls-like showdown.
- Penalty Kick Showdown: Football-themed challenge testing timing and aim.
- Casino: Select a subject and difficulty to earn higher rewards in a quiz.
- Russian Roullete: play a realistic russian roullete game to earn money.
- Interactive Event Feedback: UI-driven event displays keep players informed and engaged.
- Modular and Expandable: A clean, component-based structure means new experiences can be added as the game grows.
