REKTon Bossfight

Stakeholders:Personal project

Date Finished:Januari 2023

Languages:Unity/C#

GitHub: REKTon repo

Overview

This project marks my first venture into Unity development, serving as an immersive learning tool to understand Unity's capabilities. I designed and built a complete first-person shooter level heavily inspired by the atmospheric intensity of souls-like games.
The player progresses through a misty forest, entering a grand villa. Along the way, they confront various enemy mobs before facing off in a climactic boss battle in the garden against “REKTon". The experience is structured to combine environmental build-up with engaging combat mechanics.

Features

- Full FPS Level in Unity: Built from the ground up using Unity, this project showcases an entire playable environment complete with navigation, enemy spawning, and interaction systems.
- Atmospheric Design: Players traverse a mist-shrouded forest leading into a dark, mysterious villa, laying the foundation for tension and immersion.
- Combat Progression: The player battles several enemy mobs, with difficulty escalating as they approach the final confrontation.
- Phase 1 Bossfight: REKTon remains protected by a shield and attacks from a distance with special projectiles. This phase offers the player strategic breathing space to reload weapons and regain health.
- Phase 2 Bossfight: Once REKTon’s shield breaks, the player deals damage until the boss transitions to full aggression. In this phase, REKTon unleashes aggressive melee attacks and utilizes roll-catch tactics, demanding high spatial awareness and quick reflexes from the player.
- Gameplay Mechanics: The boss battle emphasizes dynamic threat management, combining ranged pressure with sudden close-quarters danger, encouraging players to master timing, cover usage, and movement.
